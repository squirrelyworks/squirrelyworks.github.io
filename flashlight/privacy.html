Privacy Policy

Utility Flashlight, by Squirrely Works
Effective November 25, 2026

In short: Squirrely Works does not collect, sell or share any personal information. Your settings and Morse code messages stay on your phone. The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure that ad, as described below.

What the app saves on your phone

  • Your settings, such as brightness, speeds, screen color, auto-off time and the switches in Options & Settings
  • The last Morse code message you typed, so it's there the next time you open the app

This information never leaves your phone. Squirrely Works cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include it in your phone's own backup to your Google Account; that backup is made and stored by Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows one banner ad, on the Options & Settings screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your phone's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about your phone, such as its model and Android version
  • how you interact with the ad, such as whether it was shown or tapped

This information is collected and used by Google, not by Squirrely Works. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any personalized ads.
  • Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone's settings, usually under Settings, Privacy, Ads.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time "Remove ads" upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Squirrely Works does not receive or store your payment information.

Permissions

  • Flashlight: the app turns your phone's flash on and off. It does not take photos or video and does not need camera permission.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when the light turns on or off. You can turn this off in Options & Settings.
  • Notifications and foreground service: keep the light and its effects running while the screen is off or another app is open, and show a "Flashlight on" notification.
  • Prevent the phone from sleeping: so flashing effects and Morse code keep accurate timing with the screen off.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Google's ad software, to load the ad.
  • Advertising ID: used only by Google's ad software, as described above.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Other features

  • Screen light: lights up your screen in the color and brightness you choose. Nothing about it leaves your phone.
  • Share: opens your phone's share menu with a link to the app on Google Play. The app does not see who you share it with.
  • Rate this app: opens the app's page in Google Play.
  • Privacy policy: opens this page in your phone's web browser.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and Squirrely Works does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
