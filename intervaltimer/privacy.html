Privacy Policy

Interval Timer Made Easy, by Squirrely Works
Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Interval Timer Made Easy does not collect, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no ads, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything the app saves stays on your phone.

What the app saves on your phone

  • Your saved presets and Quick Start values
  • Your settings, such as sounds, voice cues, colors and volume
  • Your workout history, used for the weekly totals: when each workout ended and how much work and rest time it had
  • The workout in progress, so it can carry on if Android closes the app

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to move it, as described below. Squirrely Works cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Backups

  • Export: when you tap Export in Settings, the app saves a file with your presets, settings and workout history to a place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your phone's own backup to your Google Account. That backup is made and stored by Google under your account and is covered by Google's privacy policy. Squirrely Works has no access to it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the workout card with the time left and Pause and Cancel buttons. This is optional; the timer works without it.
  • Foreground service: keeps the countdown and the cues running while the screen is off or another app is open.
  • Prevent the phone from sleeping: so the phone doesn't pause the timer in the middle of a workout.
  • Change audio settings: used by the beep and voice cues.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files, and it has no internet access.

Other features

  • Voice cues use the speech engine already on your phone. The app only gives it the cue words, such as "Work" and "Rest".
  • Lowering other audio: the app asks Android to turn other apps' sound down briefly during a cue. It cannot see what you are listening to.
  • Share: opens your phone's share menu with a link to the app on Google Play. The app does not see who you share it with.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles data differently (for example, if it shows ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
